About the club

The club Westford grew up at.

WestFit sits on Littleton Road with tennis and pickleball courts, pools, and a full fitness floor. It is the kind of club where a family joins for swim lessons and is still here ten years later for junior tennis and the summer swim team.

Between Dolphin Swim School, the Scorpions, junior tennis, GroupFit and summer camp, there is a version of this club for pretty much everyone in the house.
